## Decisions
- Round trip: `parseCsv` is not changed; a neutralised cell comes back with its leading `'` (inert text). Stripping it on import would turn a legit value such as `'=x` into something else; same behaviour as productos.new.
- The declared sizes in the ZIP header can lie; JSZip verifies sizes while inflating, and the row/column caps catch the rest. Not a perfect streaming limit.
- Caps are local constants in `spreadsheet.ts` (not imported from `src/domain`) to keep the lib layer independent; 5001 matches `MAX_FILAS_PLANILLA` + header.
